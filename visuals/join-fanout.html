Brightcart, an online grocery, joined orders to saved addresses to get revenue by city. Last month's report came out too high. Here you can watch it happen on five real customers from the lesson data, then fix it.

1. The two tables

A join pairs every order with every address that has the same customer_id. If each customer has one address, the key is one to one on the address side and each order appears once. Tick a box to give a customer a second saved address, and the address side becomes one to many.
