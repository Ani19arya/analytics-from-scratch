## Checks before you send

- Every number in your recommendation appears in your step 9 output or is labelled as an assumption.
- The recommendation says what NOT to do (drop carrier B, add packers), because both are proposed.
- On time is judged on dates, not timestamps, and any chart with a cut axis says so.

## A two-point answer, in short

"Ask the carrier to restore South's 20:00 collection this week; it moved to about 16:00 on 17 August, and parcels packed in those four hours now wait a day. Until it is back, South packs late-morning orders first. Do not drop carrier B (unchanged at North, 91.9%) and do not add packers (pack time unchanged). Restoring the collection would have kept on time at about 93% instead of 86.5%, about 9 fewer late parcels a week; at an assumed one contact per three late parcels, GBP 4 a contact and a GBP 3.99 refunded fee, about GBP 24 a week, plus repeat orders we cannot measure here. Caveats: the collection time is inferred from scan times, and about 1.2 points of the drop remain unexplained."
