The test checks your numbers (steps 7 to 9). This rubric checks what you do with them. Score each row 0, 1 or 2. A total of 5 or 6 is ready to send to Amara; 3 or 4 needs one more pass; below 3, start again from your step 9.

| Step | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |

## Checks before you send

- Every number in your recommendation appears in your step 9 output or is labelled as an assumption.
- The recommendation says what NOT to do (the across-the-board cut, the price rollback), because both are on the table.
- You aggregated spend and acquisitions to the same grain before joining, and divided totals rather than averaging weekly CACs.

## A two-point answer, in short

"Do not cut a third of paid spend: 23.5 of the 34.6 points are an iOS tracking break since 6 July, and search and video cost what they did in January. Growth engineering fixes the iOS social attribution this sprint; until then finance reads the corrected CAC (47, up 11%). Cap social at its Q2 level of about 91,000 a quarter, which saves about 27,000; at a marginal 78 per social subscriber against about 39 in search (assuming search does not saturate), moving that money buys about 350 more subscribers a quarter. Caveats: we assumed the lost iOS subscribers were social, and marginal CAC compares quarters; a four-week regional spend test would confirm the saturation."
