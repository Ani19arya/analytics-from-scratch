# Land the job

Level 4 turns the course on your own job search. You treat it like any other analysis: real postings as data, a clear question, honest filters, a funnel you measure, and a plan you review each week. Phase 07 prepared you for the interviews; this phase is about getting them.

The real numbers in this phase come from two places, each with its date: the monthly open data drop in [data/job-market](../../data/job-market/) and Real Analyst Jobs data retrieved on 31 October 2026. Everything else is synthetic and says so. Every lesson runs offline on Python 3.9 or later with the standard library only.

This phase uses Real Analyst Jobs (https://realanalystjobs.com), the site whose open data the course already uses, as one source among several. It is not the only place to look, and the lessons say where LinkedIn, Indeed or a company's own careers page are better.

Lessons in order. Levels: 4 land the job (see the [learning roadmap](../../LEARNING_ROADMAP.md)).

1. Build a target list from real postings (planned)
2. Search smarter: titles and synonyms (planned)
3. Visa-aware search: H-1B, OPT and UK sponsors (planned)
4. Remote, really: where can you be hired from? (planned)
5. Apply where it counts (planned)
6. Track your search as a funnel (planned)
7. Using Real Analyst Jobs in your search (guide, planned)
8. Capstone: a 30-day job search plan (planned)

Planned lessons are listed in [curriculum.json](../../curriculum.json) and appear here as links once they exist and pass their tests.

The course builds skills you can demonstrate. It cannot promise a job, and no job board can either.
