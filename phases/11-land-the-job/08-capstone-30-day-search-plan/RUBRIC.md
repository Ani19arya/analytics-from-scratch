# Rubric: capstone 11-08, a 30-day job search plan

The test checks the parts with one right answer. This rubric scores everything else in [DELIVERABLES.md](DELIVERABLES.md). Score each row 0, 1 or 2. Out of 14: 12 or more and Kavya can start on Monday; 8 to 11 needs one more pass; below 8, go back to the filters and the hours.

| Area | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Framing | A list of postings, not a plan | A plan, but it does not answer one of Kavya's four needs (days and hours, eligibility, nothing old or dodgy, a weekly check) | Opens with the plan in two sentences and answers all four needs, in her order |
| 2. Filters | Counts "remote" as open to India, or "not stated" sponsorship as a yes | The rules are applied but the note does not say how many postings each one removed | Each filter is named with the count it removed, from the data, and the reposts of roles she already applied to are called out |
| 3. Prioritising | Applications in posting order, or random | A ranking, but not explained, or it ignores her level (two years) and her tools | A stated order that a reader can check: her level first, then tool match, then closing dates, with the reason for each and what was traded away (for example, Senior roles moved to the back, not removed) |
| 4. Time | Hours do not add up, or more than 10 a week | Within budget, but every hour goes to applications | Within budget, with time kept for referrals, the weekly review and a search refresh, and a sentence on why that split |
| 5. Weekly review | No review, or "see how it goes" | A review with the numbers but no decision attached | A template with the funnel keys, the week-1 baseline filled in, and one decision rule per number, with a minimum sample before acting |
| 6. Honesty about the numbers | Treats 2 screens out of 4 referrals as "referrals work 50% of the time" | Mentions sample size once | Says plainly that one week of 17 applications cannot rank channels, gives the counts next to every rate, and says what the plan cannot promise (a reply, an interview, a job) |
| 7. Communication | Longer than a page, or a wall of rules | One page, but Kavya would need to read the code to follow it | One page in plain words, every number traceable to `plan.json` or the funnel, and a calendar she can follow without the code |

## Checks before you send

- No application to a remote posting that does not name India, APAC or worldwide.
- No application abroad unless the posting itself says it sponsors.
- No posting older than 30 days, closed, or red-flagged; no role twice; no role from last week's log.
- Every week adds up to 10 hours or less; the last two days to 2.86 or less.
- The week-1 funnel counts each application once and treats `phone_screen` and `screen` as one stage.
- Nothing in the note promises a job.
